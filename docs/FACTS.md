# Verified fact sheet — every number the pitch is allowed to use

Every stat below was adversarially fact-checked against its primary source
(July 24, 2026). Use the **citable phrasing** verbatim; the "don't say"
column is what died in verification. If a number isn't on this sheet, it
doesn't go on a slide.

## The clinical case

| # | Citable phrasing | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Five-year relative survival for colorectal cancer is **91.3% when localized vs 16.9% when distant** (SEER, 2016–2022). Say "91% vs 17%" — not 15%. | [SEER Stat Facts](https://seer.cancer.gov/statfacts/html/colorect.html) |
| 2 | Colorectal cancer incidence in adults **20–49 is rising ~3% per year**; CRC is now the **#1 cause of cancer death under 50**. | [ACS / Siegel, CRC Statistics 2026](https://acsjournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.3322/caac.70067) |
| 3 | **3 in 4 colorectal cancers in adults under 50 are diagnosed at an advanced stage** (regional or distant); screening prevalence in that age group is only ~37%. | [ACS](https://www.cancer.org/research/acs-research-news/colorectal-cancer-drops-in-older-adults-and-rises-in-young-ones.html) |
| 4 | **Every four weeks of cancer treatment delay is associated with a 6–13% increase in mortality** (surgery HR 1.06–1.08; radiotherapy up to 1.23 cervix adjuvant; 34 studies, 1.27M patients). Phrase per **four weeks**, not per week. | [Hanna et al., BMJ 2020;371:m4087](https://www.bmj.com/content/371/bmj.m4087) |
| 5 | Rule thresholds: **CAPER study, 2,093 real primary-care patients** — bleeding PPV 2.4%, weight loss 1.2%, pain 1.1%, Hb<10 2.3%, abnormal rectal exam 4.0%, positive FOB 7.1%; **any second feature raises risk to the investigation threshold**. | [Hamilton et al., Br J Cancer 2005](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2361578/) |

## The operational / buyer case

| # | Citable phrasing | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 6 | In a study of **103,737 specialty-referral scheduling attempts** at a large US academic health system, **only 34.8% resulted in documented completed appointments** — roughly two-thirds never converted to a documented visit. | [Patel et al., J Gen Intern Med 2018](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11606-018-4392-z) |
| 7 | The average wait for a new-patient **gastroenterology appointment is 40 days — the longest of any specialty surveyed** (15 US metros, range 1–208 days); that's just the consult before the colonoscopy. | [AMN Healthcare 2025 survey](https://www.amnhealthcare.com/siteassets/amn-insights/physician/ps-2025-physician-appt-wait-times---wp-v6.pdf) |

| 9 | A fully **manual prior auth takes staff an average of 24 minutes** (range 2–60) and costs the provider **$12.88 per transaction** vs $5.38 electronic (CAQH Index 2024). | [CAQH Index 2024](https://www.caqh.org/hubfs/Index/2024%20Index%20Report/CAQH_IndexReport_2024_FINAL.pdf) |

## Don't say (killed or weakened in verification)

- ~~"Phone tag is the **leading** cause of referral leakage"~~ → unable-to-contact was **6%** of referred patients (+12% declined for unclear reasons). Say: *"failure to reach the patient is a documented, addressable leak point"* — the bigger drivers are documentation gaps and fragmentation.
- ~~"45 minutes per PA phone call"~~ → 45–60 min is the top of the CAQH range, not the average. Say **24 minutes average, up to an hour**.
- ~~"91% vs 15% survival"~~ → **91% vs 17%** (SEER 2016–2022).
- ~~"1.2–3.2% mortality increase per week of delay"~~ → the paper reports **per four weeks: 6–13%**. Don't convert to per-week on a slide.
- ~~"Missed CRC is among the most common of ALL malpractice claims"~~ → verified only as the **costliest of the four cancers CRICO analyzed**. Also drop "receiving practices share liability" unless separately sourced — no verified support.
- Wait times are **averages, not medians**; the 34.8% conversion figure is one academic system, 2015–16, cross-specialty — say "in a large academic health system," not "nationally."
